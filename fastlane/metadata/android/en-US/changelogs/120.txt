Network Survey 2.0 rebuilds the whole app.
* One survey with one Start and Stop: pick what to record and where it goes
* Save setups and switch with one tap
* Four tabs: Survey, Signals, Map, More
* One Map tab with your survey points (the Cellular map button is gone)
* Rebuilt Cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and GNSS screens
* Light and dark themes, accent colors, new icon
* New MDM keys for record types and destinations
* GeoPackage logging removed; log files are CSV
